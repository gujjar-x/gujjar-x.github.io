Why this structure is different

This version avoids the typical card-dashboard appearance and uses an editorial / newspaper structure: masthead at the top, a live ticker, one strong headline area, a flat market edition, a ledger-style Today/Yesterday table and a dark “Record Room” for old charts. The final version can keep the same visual identity while connecting the live result engine and 2014–2026 backend data.
